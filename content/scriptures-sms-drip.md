# Scriptures for the Hard Days, SMS drip

Short text messages for the encouragement series that follows the instant pack.
Build this in Brevo as an SMS automation triggered when a contact is added to
the SMS list (BREVO_SMS_LIST_ID). One message every few days. Each is kept short
so it lands as one or two SMS segments. KJV, no em dashes, American spelling.

The instant text (sent by the Netlify function on submit) is separate; these are
the follow-ups.

---

Day 1
AJNC: When you are weary, hear this. "Come unto me, all ye that labour and are
heavy laden, and I will give you rest." Matthew 11:28. We are praying for you.

Day 3
AJNC: For the fearful night. "Fear thou not; for I am with thee." Isaiah 41:10.
You are not alone. Reply if we can pray with you.

Day 6
AJNC: When you feel broken. "He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up
their wounds." Psalm 147:3. God is near to you today.

Day 9
AJNC: For the anxious heart. "Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for
you." 1 Peter 5:7. Give Him the weight you are carrying.

Day 12
AJNC: You are welcome here. We gather Sunday 10 AM and 3 PM. Come as you are, or
send us a prayer request anytime: https://ajnc.ph/#prayer . Magkahiusa.
