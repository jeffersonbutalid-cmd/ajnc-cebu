# Knowing Jesus: five short letters

A 5-part welcome series for seekers who sign up (for example from the
"Scriptures for the Hard Days" page or the newsletter). Warm, plain, bold,
apostolic. KJV scripture, citations in caps, no em dashes, American spelling.

## How to load this
- Use a free email tool that supports automations (MailerLite or Brevo both
  have free tiers). Formspree only collects the address, it does not drip.
- Create an automation triggered on "subscriber added" (or tag `seeker`).
- One letter per day for five days. Each letter below has a Subject and a
  Preheader. Paste the body as plain, readable HTML or text.
- Optional merge field: replace "friend" with `{{name|friend}}` if your tool
  supports a fallback.
- Keep the AJNC voice. Do not add em dashes. American spelling only.

## Letter 1 of 5

**Subject:** Who is Jesus, really?
**Preheader:** One God, and His name is the whole story.

Hi friend,

Thank you for letting us write to you. Over five short letters, we want to show
you the heart of the gospel, plainly.

Start here. There is one God. Not three persons, one God, who has revealed
Himself as Father, as Son, and as the Holy Spirit. And when God came to save us,
He came in flesh, in Jesus.

*"For in him dwelleth all the fulness of the Godhead bodily."*
COLOSSIANS 2:9

That is why His name carries so much weight. Jesus is not one name among many. It
is the name of the one God who came to find you.

Sit with one question today. If Jesus is God with us, what would it change to
truly know Him?

Tomorrow we will talk about the first step toward Him, a clean start.

Salamat. Magkahiusa.
The AJNC Cebu family

## Letter 2 of 5

**Subject:** A clean start
**Preheader:** You do not have to clean yourself up first.

Hi friend,

Yesterday we said God came to save you in Jesus. Today, the first step toward
Him: repentance.

Repentance is not punishment. It is a turning. You stop walking your own way and
you turn to face God, honestly, exactly as you are. You do not have to fix
yourself first. You come, and He does the cleaning.

*"Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out."*
ACTS 3:19

Blotted out means gone. Not managed, not hidden, gone.

Today, tell God the truth about one thing. No speech, just honesty. He is not
waiting to shame you. He is waiting to forgive you.

Tomorrow, the step that surprises a lot of people: baptism in the name of Jesus.

Salamat. Magkahiusa.
The AJNC Cebu family

## Letter 3 of 5

**Subject:** Why we baptize in Jesus' name
**Preheader:** It is the way the first church did it.

Hi friend,

When the very first crowd asked the apostles, "What shall we do?", this was the
answer they were given.

*"Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name
of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the
Holy Ghost."*
ACTS 2:38

Notice the name. Not a formula, the name of Jesus. Every baptism in the book of
Acts was done this way (Acts 8:16, Acts 10:48, Acts 19:5). It is a burial and a
new life. You go down with Christ, and you come up washed and forgiven.

This is not a ritual to earn God's love. It is how you receive the cleansing He
already paid for at the cross.

Have you ever been baptized in the name of Jesus Christ? If not, we would love to
talk with you about it.

Tomorrow, the promise that makes all of this come alive: the Holy Ghost.

Salamat. Magkahiusa.
The AJNC Cebu family

## Letter 4 of 5

**Subject:** The promise of the Holy Ghost
**Preheader:** God does not only forgive you, He fills you.

Hi friend,

The gospel is not only that your sins can be forgiven. It is that God Himself
wants to live in you.

On the day of Pentecost, this is what happened.

*"And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other
tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance."*
ACTS 2:4

That same gift is for you. We teach what the Bible shows: the infilling of the
Holy Ghost, with the initial evidence of speaking in other tongues, just as the
first church received it. It is not strange, and it is not earned. It is the
promise.

*"For the promise is unto you, and to your children, and to all that are afar
off, even as many as the Lord our God shall call."*
ACTS 2:39

You are one of the "afar off." The promise has your name on it.

Tomorrow, the simplest letter of all: come and see.

Salamat. Magkahiusa.
The AJNC Cebu family

## Letter 5 of 5

**Subject:** Come and see
**Preheader:** There is a seat kept for you this Sunday.

Hi friend,

Over these letters we have walked through the heart of it. One God, revealed in
Jesus. A clean start through repentance. Baptism in the name of Jesus Christ. The
gift of the Holy Ghost.

None of it was meant to be read alone. It was meant to be lived, with a family.

So here is our invitation, plainly. Come and see.

*"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you
rest."*
MATTHEW 11:28

We gather in Mandaue on Sunday at 10 AM and 3 PM. Come as you are. No dress code,
no pressure, a seat already kept for you. If you would rather we reach out first,
just reply to this email, or send a prayer request and a pastor will pray with
you this week.

Whatever you are carrying, you do not have to carry it alone.

Salamat. Magkahiusa.
The AJNC Cebu family
