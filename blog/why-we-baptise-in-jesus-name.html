Why we baptise in Jesus' name.

Acts 2:38 is not a footnote in our faith. It is the door. Here is what Peter preached, and why we still preach it in Mandaue.

Pastor Mario Doromal · 10 May 2026 · 6 min read

On the day of Pentecost, Peter stood up with the eleven and preached. The crowd was cut to the heart and asked the question every honest person eventually asks. "Men and brethren, what shall we do?" Peter did not give a vague answer. He gave a specific one. He gave the same one we still give in Casuntingan, on the second floor of Un Heng Building, every time someone walks up after the service and asks what to do next.

"Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost." Acts 2:38

That is the door. Repentance. Baptism in the name of Jesus Christ. The infilling of the Holy Ghost. Three things, one gospel, preached by a man who had just been filled with the Spirit in the upper room. He did not invent it. He repeated what Jesus had told them to do.

But what about Matthew 28:19?

It is the question every honest family asks, and it deserves an honest answer. Jesus said, "Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost." Peter heard those words. So did John, and James, and the rest. They were standing on the mountain when He said it.

And then, ten days after He ascended, Peter stood up and obeyed. He named the name. Father, Son, and Holy Ghost are not three names. They are titles. The name that fills them all is Jesus. The Son's name is Jesus. The Father gave the Son His name (John 5:43). The Spirit comes in His name (John 14:26). One name fills the formula, and the apostles knew it.

That is why the book of Acts never records a single trinitarian baptism. It records baptism in the name of Jesus, again and again, from Pentecost in Jerusalem to a Roman household in Caesarea to a riverbank in Philippi to a synagogue in Ephesus.

"Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved." Acts 4:12

There is one name. The name above every name. And that name is Jesus.

What baptism actually does.

Baptism is not a public ceremony. It is not a graduation. It is a burial. Paul says so plainly. "We are buried with him by baptism into death." The waters are a grave. We go down in them because the old life has ended. We come up because a new one has started. The remission of sins is real. The Spirit fills the empty room.

If you have been considering this step, family, the waters are open. There is no fee. There is no test. There is no waiting list. Pastor or one of the brothers will sit with you for as long as you need. We have done this for forty years in Mandaue and we will do it again this Sunday if you ask.

Come as you are. We have kept a seat for you.

Written by

Pastor Mario Doromal

Church Pastor of AJNC Cebu, shepherding the family on the 2nd floor of Un Heng Building, Casuntingan since 1986. Husband, father, preacher of Acts 2:38.
