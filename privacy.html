Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

Apostolic Jesus Name Church (AJNC) Cebu respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect when you use this website, why, and how to reach us about it. We keep this short and plain on purpose.

What we collect

  • Prayer requests and newsletter sign-ups. When you send a prayer request or sign up for letters, we receive what you type (for example your name, email, and message). These forms are handled by Formspree, which delivers them to our pastoral team.
  • Chat with Grace. If you use the assistant, the messages you type are sent to our server to generate a reply. Do not share sensitive personal details in the chat. For anything private or urgent, contact a pastor directly.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our host may log standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

What we do with it

  • Read and pray over the requests you send, and reply when you ask us to.
  • Send the church letter only to people who sign up for it.
  • Keep the site safe and working.

We do not sell your information, and we do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Maps, video, and links

Some pages embed third-party services (for example a map provider, YouTube, or Formspree). When these load, those providers may receive standard request data under their own privacy policies.

Keeping and removing your information

We keep prayer requests and sign-ups only as long as we need them to serve you. You can ask us to remove your information at any time by emailing hello@ajnc.ph.

Children

This site is for general church information and is not directed at collecting personal data from children. If a child has sent us information, a parent or guardian can contact us to have it removed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@ajnc.ph or call +63 917 555 4673. You can also visit us at 2nd Floor, Un Heng Building, Casuntingan, Mandaue City, Cebu.
