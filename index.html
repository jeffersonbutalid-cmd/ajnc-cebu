Who we are

A family carrying the gospel here in Mandaue.

AJNC Cebu is a Oneness Pentecostal family on the 2nd floor of Un Heng Building, Casuntingan. One congregation. One body. Rooted in Mandaue since 1986.

We hold the apostolic faith plainly. One God, revealed in Jesus Christ. Baptism in His name for the remission of sins. The infilling of the Holy Ghost. The gospel of Pentecost is the gospel we preach in Mandaue.

Our story is small and ordinary. Families who pray together on Sunday and on Wednesday. Neighbours invited to dinner. Baptisms in the sea. Prayer meetings that run later than they were supposed to. Together, those small things have become a family in Casuntingan.

What we believe

The apostolic faith,
plain and unhidden.

Six pillars we have not, and will not, compromise on.

01

One God

There is one God who has revealed Himself as Father in creation, Son in redemption, Holy Ghost in regeneration. Deuteronomy 6:4.

02

Jesus is Lord

In Him dwells all the fullness of the Godhead bodily. He is the Almighty, the I AM, the Saviour of the world. Colossians 2:9.

03

Acts 2:38

Repent, and be baptised in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins — and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost.

04

The Holy Ghost

Infilling of the Spirit, evidenced by speaking in other tongues as the Spirit gives utterance. The Pentecost experience, today.

05

The Word

The Bible is the inspired Word of God — sufficient, infallible, the standard for faith, practice, and the church we are building.

06

The Family

The church is the household of God. We walk together across generations and neighbourhoods. Nobody walks alone.

Church Pastor & leadership

A family shepherded.

The pastor and elders who shepherd the AJNC Cebu family.

Church Pastor

Bro. Mario Doromal

Church Pastor, AJNC Cebu

"That I may know him, and the power of his resurrection, and the fellowship of his sufferings."

Philippians 3:10

Bro. Mario shepherds the AJNC Cebu family on the 2nd floor of Un Heng Building, Casuntingan. His ministry is shaped by the apostolic doctrine of Acts 2:38, a deep love for the local family, and an unhurried commitment to the Word.

He preaches every Sunday at the Mandaue church and walks with the family through every ordinary day in between.

A life faithfully lived

Rev. Emilio Espera Jr.

10.09.1975  to  03.07.2026

Founding pastor of AJNC Cebu. Pastor Espera went home to be with the Lord in March 2026 after a lifetime of faithful service. The family carries his work forward.
