Church Pastor

Bro. Mario Doromal.

Shepherd of the AJNC Cebu family in Mandaue.

Bro. Mario shepherds the AJNC Cebu family on the 2nd floor of Un Heng Building, Casuntingan. His ministry is shaped by the apostolic doctrine of Acts 2:38, a deep love for the Word, and a pastoral heart for every family in the room.

He preaches every Sunday at the Mandaue church and walks with the family through every ordinary day in between. Whatever you're carrying, there's a seat at the table.
